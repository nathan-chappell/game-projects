welcome, world maker

This is your little adventure.

  1. Explore. Click the game. Walk with the arrow keys or W A S D. Enter the second room to meet Flowey. Face a nearby object and press C to inspect it. Before meeting Flowey, you get a short description; afterward, he tells you not to waste time. C, Z or Enter advances dialogue. Press L to skip ordinary conversations. Important Cutscenes are labeled “I. C.” and cannot be skipped with L. Finish his speech and he joins you. Continue through the right-hand door into the Echo Chamber for your next conversation. Follow the glowing cracks through its right-hand door to find the Dark Fountain. Continue the conversation to see Susie’s Rude Buster and bring everyone into your party. Then face the fountain and press C or Z for the next I. C.: jumping into dark.G.world. After landing, explore its black passage with your whole party.
  2. Build. Pick a tile and paint. Place Flowey in room 2, choose your starting spot, or write new dialogue. Use Place NPC to add Froggit; select it to edit its name and speech. Face it and press C or Z to talk. Doors connect the rooms you choose.
  3. Keep it. “Download game” saves a new HTML file with all your edits. Double-click it to play again, even offline.

This file has a map editor, not a built-in AI chatbot. For bigger changes, describe what you want in our chat. Nothing in this game is sent anywhere.

Walking and talking also work with the buttons below the game. Browser storage may remember edits, but downloading a copy is the reliable way to keep them.
